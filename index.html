About the League

COBL x Street 11 Basketball is a recreational basketball league in Singapore built by players, for players. More than just a place to compete, COBL is a community where passion for basketball brings people together, friendships are built, and brotherhood grows both on and off the court.

We believe every player deserves the opportunity to compete, improve, and be recognized. That’s why we bring every game day to life with live scoreboards, real-time statistics, and broadcast-style coverage, capturing every play, every milestone, and every moment that makes the game special.

From the opening tip-off to the final buzzer, every game matters. Every player has a story, every team has something to prove, and every point counts.

COBL is more than basketball. It’s about community, competition, and brotherhood.

Tournament Format

From a single round-robin to best-of-3 finals: every team plays every other team, then the top 8 fight through a best-of-3 playoff bracket for the Season 3 crown.

Stage 1

Single Group Round-Robin

  • All 11 teams in one group, each playing every other team once
  • 55 total games
  • Top 8 advance to the playoffs
  • Bottom 3 are eliminated
Stage 2

Playoff Bracket

  • Quarterfinals and semifinals are best-of-3
  • The league championship is a best-of-3 finals
  • Losing semifinalists meet in a single-game 3rd place match
